Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for Sutured

Date: October 3, 2026

Thank you for using Sutured ("the App"). This document outlines our commitment to your privacy and the terms you agree to when using the App.

Delete your account

  1. In the app (instant). Open the App, go to Settings, tap Delete Account, and enter your PIN to confirm. Your account and data are deleted immediately and cannot be recovered.
  2. Without the app. If you no longer have the App installed or cannot sign in, email contact@wrklabs.in with the subject "Delete my Sutured account" and include your username. We will delete your account and confirm by reply within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

  1. Deleted. Your account and login, your display name, your spaced-repetition progress, your answer history, and any content flags you submitted.
  2. Kept. Results of 1v1 Quiz Arena games you played remain in your opponent's game history so their record stays correct. Your name on those games is replaced with "Player" and they are no longer linked to any account. Nothing else about you is kept, and there is no additional retention period.

Delete only your study progress

  1. Keep your account. In the App, go to Settings and tap Reset Study Data, then confirm with your PIN. This deletes your spaced-repetition progress and answer history, on your device and on our servers, and keeps your account.

Privacy Policy

  1. Account Registration. To access certain features of the App—such as syncing official materials and participating in the 1v1 Quiz Arena—you must register for an account. Registration does not require an email address; you choose a username and set a 5-digit PIN, and we store these credentials to authenticate your identity, manage your account, and host your online gameplay profile. We do not sell or rent your account information to third parties.
  2. Display Name. To play in the 1v1 Quiz Arena you choose a display name, which is shown to your opponents and is separate from your login username. Game lobbies are visible only to the two players in them.
  3. Officially Provided Content. All questions (MCQs) and notes available in the App are officially curated and provided by us. Users can no longer create, upload, or store their own custom questions, notes, or tags.
  4. Cloud-Synced Study Progress. Once you sign in, your study and review activity—including which questions you've answered, your correctness history, and spaced-repetition scheduling data—is synced to and stored on our servers so it is available across your devices. The App also communicates with our servers to download official quiz questions and notes, and to synchronize real-time multiplayer states.
  5. Real-Time Multiplayer Data (1v1 Quiz Arena). When you use the 1v1 Quiz Arena feature, the App transmits live gameplay inputs, including your speed, score updates, and answers, to facilitate real-time matches with your opponent. We store match records (such as game lobby IDs, final scores, and the winner's user ID) on our servers to maintain matchmaking integrity and user stats.
  6. Deleting Your Data. You can delete your account at any time in the App (Settings, Delete Account), or request deletion by email, as described on our account deletion section at the top of this page. Deleting your account removes your login, display name, study progress, answer history and content flags. Results of Arena games you played remain in your opponent's history, with your name replaced by "Player". You can also delete only your study progress with Settings, Reset Study Data.
  7. Third-Party Services. We use Supabase as our backend-as-a-service provider to handle secure authentication, database storage, and real-time matchmaking communication. Your credentials, study progress, and online gameplay statistics are stored securely on Supabase servers in compliance with their privacy and security protocols.
  8. No Ads or Trackers. The App is free of advertisements and third-party advertising trackers. We do not track your activity outside of the App.
  9. AI-Generated Content. Some of the content in the App may be generated or drafted with the help of artificial intelligence tools. Such material can contain mistakes, so please verify anything important against authoritative sources. You can report a problem with any question or note using the flag button in the App.
  10. Educational Content Disclaimer. The App provides questions and notes for educational purposes only. It is not intended to provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Users should consult qualified medical professionals for any health-related decisions.
