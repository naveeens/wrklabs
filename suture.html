Once you sign in, your study and review activity—including which questions you've answered, your correctness history, and spaced-repetition scheduling data—is synced to and stored on our servers so it is available across your devices. We store match records (such as game lobby IDs, final scores, and the winner's user ID) on our servers to maintain matchmaking integrity and user stats.
