Privacy Policy & Terms of Service for Launcher

Last Updated: October 29, 2026

Thank you for using Launcher ("the App"), a minimalist home-screen launcher. This document outlines our commitment to your privacy and the terms you agree to when using the App.

Privacy Policy

  1. Everything Stays on Your Device. The App does not send your data anywhere. Your settings and preferences are stored only on your device. The only network traffic is the check with Google Play for the optional Pro purchase described below.
  2. Why It Can See Your Installed Apps. A launcher has to list your apps to show and open them, so the App uses Android's launcher interface to see which apps you can open. The list is used only on your device to draw your home screen and app drawer, and is never stored off the device.
  3. Other Permissions. The App asks to uninstall apps (only when you choose to uninstall one), to vibrate for feedback, and to open the notification panel.
  4. No Account. The App has no sign-in and no account with us. We do not receive any data about you.
  5. No Ads or Trackers. The App is free of advertisements and third-party trackers. We do not run analytics or crash reporting.
  6. Optional One-Time Pro Purchase. The App offers an optional one-time Pro purchase that unlocks AMOLED black and extra accent colours. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play, which tells the App whether Pro is owned. We never see or store your payment details, and we do not receive your name or email from the purchase.
  7. Data Retention. Settings live on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall it. We hold nothing, so there is nothing for us to delete.

Terms of Service

  1. Your Device, Your Responsibility. Using a launcher changes how you reach your apps. You are responsible for the apps you hide, restrict or uninstall through it.
  2. Self-Imposed Limits. Features that limit or delay opening apps are tools you set up for yourself. They are not security features and can be bypassed, for example by changing your default launcher.
  3. Uninstalling Apps. When you uninstall an app from the App, Android asks you to confirm and the app and its data are removed by the system. We cannot restore them.
  4. In-App Purchase. The optional one-time Pro purchase unlocks AMOLED black and extra accent colours. Pausing apps and every other feature are free. It is processed by Google Play and is subject to Google Play's refund policy. If a purchase is refunded or revoked, the App returns to the free look.

  6. Limitation of Liability. In no event shall the developer be liable for any damages (including missed notifications or calls, or data loss) arising from the use of or inability to use the App.
