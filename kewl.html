Privacy Policy & Terms of Service for kewl

Last Updated: October 29, 2026

Thank you for using kewl ("the App"), a quests-and-rewards app where small real-world tasks fill a jar with a collection. This document outlines our commitment to your privacy and the terms you agree to when using the App.

Privacy Policy

  1. Your Data is Stored Locally. Your quests, coins, collection and settings are stored only on your device. We do not have access to this data, and it is never sent to our servers.
  2. No Account, No Internet. The App has no sign-in and does not use the internet. We do not receive any data about you.
  3. Notifications. If you allow it, the App sends local reminders from your device. They are scheduled on the device and involve no server.
  4. Sensors. The accelerometer is read on the device to tilt and shake the jar. Its readings are never stored or sent.
  5. No Ads or Trackers. The App is free of advertisements and third-party trackers. We do not run analytics or crash reporting.
  6. Data Retention. Everything lives on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall it. We hold nothing, so there is nothing for us to delete.

  2. Not a Game of Chance for Money. Coins and collectibles have no cash value and cannot be bought, sold or exchanged for money or anything else.
  3. Do Safe Things. Quests are suggestions for small, worthwhile real-world actions. Use your own judgment, and never do anything unsafe, unlawful or harmful to yourself or others to complete one.
  4. Back Up Your Data. Your data is stored only on your device, and we cannot recover it if you lose the device or uninstall the App.
