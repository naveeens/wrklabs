Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for Where at

Date: September 12, 2026

Thank you for using Where at ("the App"). This document outlines our commitment to your privacy and the terms you agree to when using the App.

Privacy Policy

  1. Your Data is Stored Locally. Your tags, tagged time blocks, and settings are stored only on your device. We do not have access to this data, and it is never sent to our servers — the App has no account and no cloud sync.
  2. We Collect No Personal Information. The App does not require an account, email, or phone number, and does not run its own analytics or crash reporting.
  3. Ads. The App shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as an advertising ID) and usage data to serve and measure ads, governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can review your ad personalization settings in your device's Google Settings.
  4. In-App Purchases. Purchases ("Remove Ads", "Unlock Premium") are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details.
  5. Permissions We Use. "Display over other apps" shows the unlock-time tagging prompt; the foreground service and notification keep tracking running reliably and show its ongoing status; "Receive boot completed" resumes tracking after a restart; the battery-optimization exemption keeps tracking from being killed in the background. None of these are used to collect or transmit data — they exist only to keep the App's own tracking working.
