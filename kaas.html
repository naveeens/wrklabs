Privacy Policy & Terms of Service for Kaas

Last Updated: September 13, 2026

Thank you for using Kaas ("the App"), a personal finance tracker. This document outlines our commitment to your privacy and the terms you agree to when using the App.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the App and go to Settings. Under "Sync Splits/Loans," tap your account tile (it shows your display name and code).
  2. Tap the delete icon. In the dialog that opens, tap the trash icon next to "Edit Account."
  3. Confirm deletion. Tap "Delete" to confirm. This immediately signs you out and removes your account, display name, and sync profile from our servers. Your local transactions, categories, and other data are kept on your device and are not affected.

Privacy Policy

  1. Most of Your Data is Stored Locally. Your transactions, categories, budgets, and other personal financial data are stored only on your device unless you explicitly share a split or loan with a contact. We do not have access to this local data, and it is never sent to our servers.
  2. Account & Display Name. To share splits and loans with a contact, you create an account with a display name — no email or phone number is required. We use Supabase, a third-party backend provider, to store your account identifier, display name, and any splits or loans you choose to share.
  3. Shared Splits & Loans Are Visible to the Other Party. When you create a split or loan with a contact, that contact can see the display name, amount, description, and status of the record you shared with them. This shared record remains part of their own reconciliation and is not retroactively removed if you edit, delete, or remove it from your side, or delete your account.
  4. Data Retention & Account Deletion. You can delete your account at any time from Settings. This removes your account and display name from our servers. Splits or loans already visible to another user are not deleted from their view, since doing so would alter their own financial records without their consent.
  5. Optional In-App Purchase. The App offers an optional, one-time "Pro" purchase to support development — it does not unlock any feature. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We use RevenueCat, a third-party purchase-management provider, to verify your purchase; RevenueCat receives your purchase/transaction details and a device-level identifier for this purpose. We never see or store your payment details (card number, billing address, etc.) — that stays with Google Play.
  6. No Ads or Trackers. The App is free of advertisements and third-party trackers. We do not run analytics or crash reporting.
